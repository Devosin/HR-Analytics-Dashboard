Project: HR Analytics Dashboard

1. Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to understand employee attrition, identify workforce retention challenges, and determine which employee groups or job roles experience the highest turnover. This dashboard consolidates key HR metrics to help HR teams monitor workforce trends and investigate potential causes of employee attrition.

2. Goal of the Dashboard

To analyze employee attrition patterns based on age, gender, education, salary, years at the company, and job role. The dashboard enables HR managers to identify high-risk employee segments and develop data-driven strategies to improve employee retention.

3. Walkthrough of Key Visuals

- KPI Cards: Display overall employees (1.47K), attrition (237), attrition rate (16.1%), average age (36.92), average salary (6.50K), and average tenure (7.01 years).
- Department Filters: Allow users to explore HR, Research & Development, and Sales departments.
- Attrition by Gender: Compares employee attrition between male and female employees.
- Attrition by Education: Shows attrition distribution across educational backgrounds, including Life Sciences, Medical, and Marketing.
- Attrition by Age: Highlights the age groups experiencing the highest attrition, with the 26–35 age group leading.
- Job Role Analysis: Identifies job roles with the highest attrition, particularly Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives.
- Attrition by Salary Slab: Shows that employees earning up to 5K account for the largest number of departures.
- Attrition by Years at Company: Illustrates how attrition varies with employee tenure, highlighting early-career retention challenges.

4. Business Impacts & Insights

- Retention Planning: Helps HR teams identify employee groups that may require targeted retention initiatives.
- Salary Optimization: Highlights the concentration of attrition among lower-paid employees, supporting compensation reviews.
- Role-Based Strategies: Identifies job roles with higher attrition for focused engagement and retention programs.
- Employee Lifecycle Management: Reveals tenure-related patterns that can guide onboarding, career development, and employee engagement.
- Data-Driven Decisions: Supports workforce planning and helps HR managers prioritize interventions based on measurable trends.

5. Tech Stack

- Power BI: Interactive dashboards and visualizations.
- Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation.
- DAX: Calculated measures, KPIs, and attrition-rate analysis.
- Data Modeling: Organizing employee data for multidimensional analysis.
